# Project artwork

`icon.png` is original AI-generated project artwork created with the built-in
OpenAI image-generation tool. It depicts an indoor security camera connected to
a local video recorder. It is not an Amazon, Blink or Unraid logo and does not
imply affiliation.

Generation prompt:

> Use case: logo-brand. One original compact app icon for a home-security-camera-to-local-recorder bridge. Show exactly two recognizable objects: on the left a small INDOOR SECURITY CAMERA with a tall rectangular front face, one black circular lens near its top, on a short pedestal stand; on the right a squat NETWORK VIDEO RECORDER box with two tiny status lights and one drive slot. A short thick mint-green right-pointing connector arrow goes from the camera to the recorder. Camera is cream with forest-green outline, recorder forest-green with cream details. Arrange objects close together in a balanced compact square icon composition, filling most of the canvas with modest outer padding. Clean flat pictogram, strong thick shapes, square corners, legible at small sizes. No photographic handheld camera, no shutter button, no viewfinder, no screens or computer monitors, no circular arrows. No text, letters, logos, gradients, shadows or textures. Transparent background. The meaning must be specifically a surveillance camera connected to a local video recorder.

The original PNG alpha channel is preserved. Project artwork is offered under
this repository's MIT license to the extent any rights subsist.
