Outdoor 4 (sedona) settings mapping, 2026-10-17

Source: locally inspected signed Blink Android 55 app, whose APK hashes and
signing identity are recorded in bridge/certificates/PROVENANCE.txt. No app
source or binaries are distributed. Mappings are independently implemented.

Reads/writes: CameraApi GET/POST
/api/v2/accounts/{account}/networks/{network}/cameras/{camera}/config
GsonModule uses LOWER_CASE_WITH_UNDERSCORES. UpdateCameraBody provides the wire
fields and @SerializedName overrides. Single-field writes preserve other values.

UI field -> wire field; allowed values; evidence:
motion_sensitivity -> motion_sensitivity; integer 1-9;
  DeviceSettingsMotionFragmentKt rangeTo(1,9), MotionViewModel saveSettings.
retrigger_time -> alert_interval; integer 10-60 seconds;
  DeviceSettingsMotionFragmentKt rangeTo(10,60), MotionViewModel saveSettings.
clip_length -> video_length; integer 5 through camera clip_max_length (up to 60);
  VideoPhotoViewModel slider conversion adds 5; CameraConfigInfo clip_max_length.
volume_control -> lfr_sync_interval; integer 1-8;
  AudioViewModel VOLUME_MIN/MAX and UpdateCameraBody @SerializedName.
led_state -> led_state; off/recording, intersect reported modes when present;
  LedState identifiers and Outdoor 4 options in Blink's official support article.
illuminator_intensity -> illuminator_intensity; 1/4/7;
  IrIntensity LOW/MEDIUM/HIGH and VideoPhotoViewModel saveSettings.
video_quality -> video_quality; saver/standard/best;
  VideoQuality identifiers. VideoQualityViewModel restrictions exclude best on
  MCS2/MCS4 and standard on MCS2 only when listed as restricted by the camera.
snapshot_period_minutes -> snapshot_period_minutes; camera-reported options only;
  CameraConfigInfo snapshot_period_minutes_options and UpdateCameraBody setter.
auto_update_thumbnail_enabled -> auto_update_thumbnail_enabled; Boolean;
  VideoPhotoViewModel setter and Gson snake-case serialization.

Official cross-checks:
https://support.blinkforhome.com/en_GB/can-i-disable-the-blue-recording-led-on-the-camera
https://support.blinkforhome.com/en_US/camera-settings/new-device-layout-settings

Existing Boolean and IR mode mappings are retained. Image flip remains gated by
flip_video_compatible. Reported spotlight brightness is not proof of an attached
supported spotlight; spotlight controls stay gated. Diagnostics such as Wi-Fi,
battery and firmware remain read-only. This change covers the existing settings
rows, not arbitrary diagnostic writes, zone editing or temperature calibration.

Verification: live read of Outdoor 4 configuration; request/read-back fixtures
for every new writable field; invalid types/ranges and missing capability tests.
No physical camera settings were changed during the mapping investigation.
